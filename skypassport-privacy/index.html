SKY PASSPORT · ANDROID GAME

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Sky Passport ("the game") is a casual airplane game. This policy explains what information is handled when you play it.

What the game itself stores

Your progress (unlocked cities, stars, coins, souvenirs) and settings (language, music, sound effects, vibration) are saved only on your device. The game has no user accounts, no chat and no sign-in, and the game itself does not send this information anywhere. Uninstalling the game or using "Reset progress" deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): optional rewarded video ads you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ads between flights. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions and diagnostic information (for example crash and performance data)

This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can learn how Google uses information from apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

  • INTERNET — to load ads.
  • VIBRATE — for optional vibration feedback (can be turned off in Settings).
